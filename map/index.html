Riyadh Metro map 2026: all lines and stations

A schematic map of all six Riyadh Metro lines: Blue Line (25 stations), Red Line (15 stations), Orange Line (22 stations), Yellow Line (9 stations), Green Line (12 stations), Purple Line (11 stations). 83 stations in all, with every interchange.

Open the interactive map

How to read the map

  • Blue Line (Line 1): SAB Bank to Ad Dar Al Baida. Stations
  • Red Line (Line 2): King Saud University to King Fahad Stadium. Stations
  • Orange Line (Line 3): Jeddah Road to Khashm Al An. Stations
  • Yellow Line (Line 4): KAFD to Airport T1-2. Stations
  • Green Line (Line 5): Ministry of Education to National Museum. Stations
  • Purple Line (Line 6): KAFD to An Naseem. Stations
  • Interchanges: Al Hamra, An Naseem, Ar Rabi, KAFD, Ministry of Education, National Museum, Qasr Al Hokm, SABIC, Uthman Bin Affan Road, stc.
  • The Yellow and Purple lines share the track and the four stations from KAFD to SABIC; on the map they run side by side there.
