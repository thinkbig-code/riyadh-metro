Riyadh Airport to Kingdom Centre by metro

From King Khalid Airport Terminals 1-2, take the Yellow Line, then the Blue Line to Kingdom Centre: about 55–71 minutes door to door with 1 change, SAR 4 for a 2-hour ticket.

55–71 min · 1 change · 6 min walking · SAR 4

Yellow Line Blue Line
