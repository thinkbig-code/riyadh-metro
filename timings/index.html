Riyadh Metro timings and fares

The Riyadh Metro runs Saturday to Thursday 05:30–00:00 and on Friday 10:00–00:00. One 2-hour ticket costs SAR 4 (first class SAR 10) with any number of rides and transfers.

These are published service hours, not last-train times. Depending on direction, the last train through a station can leave well before closing; late in the evening check the station screens or the darb app. Hours change during Ramadan and on public holidays.

Open today, tomorrow, Thursday and Friday

  • Today and tomorrow: Saturday to Thursday the metro opens at 05:30 and closes at 00:00. On Friday it opens at 10:00 and closes at 00:00.
  • Thursday: 05:30 to 00:00, the same as the rest of the week.
  • Does the metro run on Friday? Yes, from 10:00 to 00:00. It opens later than on other days.
  • Weekend: the Saudi weekend is Friday and Saturday. Saturday hours are 05:30 to 00:00.
  • Last train: 00:00 is the published closing time of the system, not a timetable. The last train from a given station can leave earlier, so check the station screen in the evening.

Trains run about every 3–5 minutes at peak times and every 5–10 minutes the rest of the day, about every 10 minutes late in the evening.

Pay with a darb card, the darb app or a contactless bank card. Trains have First Class, Family and Singles sections. Rules, fines and how to pay.
