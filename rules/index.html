Riyadh Metro rules, fines and tickets

What a new rider needs to know: which ticket to buy, how to pay at the gates, what is fined and how much, and the mistakes people make in their first week. Fines are from the national public transport regulation; check the Darb app for any change.

Fare

TicketStandardFirst class
2-hour ticketSAR 4SAR 10
3-day passSAR 20SAR 50
7-day passSAR 40SAR 100
30-day passSAR 140SAR 350

One 2-hour ticket covers any number of rides and transfers on the metro and Riyadh buses. Children under 6 travel free with an adult.

Students, older people, people with disabilities, cancer patients and first-degree relatives of martyrs get 50% off on the metro and buses. The discount is checked at a station ticket office; tapping a bank card at the gate always charges the full fare.

How to pay

  • Contactless bank card or Apple Pay: tap it at the gate, no ticket needed (full fare).
  • Darb card: a reusable card for the metro and buses, sold at station ticket machines and ticket offices; top it up at the machines.
  • Darb app: buy a ticket or pass and scan its QR code at the gate.

Amounts from the table for rail lines inside cities in the 2023 national regulation. Riding without a valid ticket, or in First Class on a standard ticket, is also fined.

Common mistakes

  • Sitting in First Class with a standard ticket.
  • Using a discounted ticket without the proof (student ID and so on) on you.
  • Tapping a bank card when you are entitled to a discount: the gate charges full fare.
  • Bringing a scooter or bicycle that does not fold.
  • Going to the wrong airport station: Airport T1-2, Airport T3-4 and Airport T5 are three different stations on the Yellow Line.
  • Arriving too early on Friday: the metro opens at 10:00 on Fridays (05:30 on other days).
