Step by step: King Khalid Airport Terminals 1-2 to Al Faisaliah Tower

60–76 min · 1 change · 6 min walking · SAR 4

Yellow Line Blue Line

  1. Go to Airport T1-2~2 min from King Khalid Airport Terminals 1-2, a short walk to the terminal and arrivals hall
  2. Take the Yellow Line towards KAFDBoard at Airport T1-2 · 8 stops · ~33 min · Get off at KAFD
  3. Change to the Blue LineFollow the Blue Line signs inside KAFD · ~4 min
  4. Take the Blue Line towards Ad Dar Al Baida9 stops · ~17 min · Get off at King Fahad Library
  5. Walk to Al Faisaliah Tower~4 min, estimated from the distance on the map
