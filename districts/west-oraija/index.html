Nearest metro station to West Oraija, Riyadh

Interactive map, opened at Ad Douh, the nearest station. Tap another station to get a route. Open full screen

West Oraija has no metro station of its own. The nearest station to the middle of the district is Ad Douh on the Orange Line, about 2.0 km away in a straight line. The nearest station with Park & Ride is Western Station, about 2.3 km away.
