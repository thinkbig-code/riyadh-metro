Nearest metro station to Al Yarmuk, Riyadh

Al Yarmuk has no metro station of its own. The nearest station to the middle of the district is Al Yarmuk on the Purple Line, about 2.5 km away in a straight line. The nearest station with Park & Ride is Al Hamra, about 3.6 km away.
